CareerRadar discovery update — 20 November 2026

Latest user decision supersedes the earlier jobs-first-homepage proposal:
- Keep the original marketing homepage at / and retain /about as an alias.
- Explore your market opens /jobs without requiring login or onboarding.
- /explore remains an alias for the public Jobs view.
- Public visitors may search/filter and inspect job requirements.
- Application links, original listing links on role pages, and available hiring
  contacts require sign-in in the interface. This is a free-account gate.
- Email/password return routing captures the original destination before async
  callbacks; Google redirect and callback URLs also preserve safe internal paths.

Pages:
/jobs — public search, city/workplace/experience/date filters, sorting, details.
/jobs/:id — direct role lookup, requirements, source dates, save/apply actions.
/companies — public company search and filters; signed-in saved-company filter.
/companies/:id — company research, official links and persistent company save.
/radar and /dashboard — existing personalized workspace and profile guard.
/applications — dedicated private tracker with manual roles, stages and notes.
/account — profile, private resume controls, Free membership and alert status.
/pricing — Free/Pro comparison; Pro is explicitly planned and cannot be bought.
Existing Clerk forms, profile setup, resume storage and legal pages remain.

Data:
- The existing research file is preserved. A compact public discovery index
  contains 9,173 company research records and is generated by
  node scripts/build-discovery-companies.mjs.
- Research records are not presented as live vacancies or current job counts.
- The public feed only shows published, sourced, open roles checked within
  14 days. Missing requirements, dates and contacts remain explicitly unknown.
- No synthetic jobs were inserted into the live feed. Browser QA fixtures exist
  only in intercepted requests inside isolated testing browser contexts.
- Migration 202611200013_saved_companies.sql adds candidate_saved_companies,
  with identity-mapped owner policies for authenticated select/insert/delete.
  It was applied to the existing Supabase project without altering resumes.

Not activated:
- Contact research/verification and monthly unlock allowances.
- Saved-search email delivery and watched-company monitoring.
- Paid membership, checkout, renewals or sponsorships.
Pricing copy identifies those as planned. No fake contact unlocks are sold.
